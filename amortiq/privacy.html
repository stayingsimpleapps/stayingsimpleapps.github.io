Amortiq Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Short version. Amortiq has no accounts and no servers. The loan details you enter stay on your device. We, the developer, never receive them. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

Amortiq ("the app") is published by Ashish Negi, trading as Staying Simple Apps, based in India ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, email stayingsimple.apps@gmail.com.

Information you enter

To calculate EMIs, compare offers and track loans, you enter details such as a loan name, amount, interest rate, tenure, start date, fees and extra payments. The app also stores your settings, such as your currency and light or dark theme.

  • All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no server and cannot see, recover or delete it for you.
  • The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, bank login, contacts, location or photos.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where allowed),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, language and app version,
  • ad interactions, such as views and taps.

Your loan details are never shared with Google or any ad network.

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice later from the app's menu. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS the app does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and you can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings.

Sharing

When you share a PDF, the app hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next (email, a messaging app, your files) is your choice and is covered by that service's privacy policy.

Reminders

EMI and extra-payment reminders are scheduled locally on your device and don't go through any server. They show the loan's name, and an extra-payment reminder also shows the planned payment amount, so this can appear on your lock screen. You can turn off or hide Amortiq notifications in your device settings.

Data we collect

We, the developer, collect no personal data. We use no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, for ads, as described above. PDF reports are built entirely on your device.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Modern Android and iOS devices also encrypt this storage when the device is locked. Use a screen lock to keep it protected, and keep your device's software up to date.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a loan in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Device or cloud backups you have turned on may keep a copy under your own backup settings. For data held by Google, use Google's tools linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or limit some processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide. Your loan data is already in your hands. We do not sell your personal information. For any request, email us at the address above.

Children

Amortiq is a finance tool meant for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Not financial advice

Amortiq is a calculator for planning. It is not a lender or financial adviser. All figures are estimates, and your lender's documents are final.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
