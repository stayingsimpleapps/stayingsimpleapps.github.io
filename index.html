How we build. Three rules every app here follows.

We make small, offline-first apps for one platform at a time. Your own data stays on your phone; the only thing we lean on anyone else for is ads. The same three rules hold whether it's a loan planner or a game.

01

Your data stays with you

Loans, settings and history live in the app's own storage, encrypted with AES-256 and locked to your device's keystore. Your loan details are never uploaded to us — we run no server, so there's nothing of yours for us to hold or lose.

02

Open it and go

No sign-up, no password, no email. There's nothing to create and nothing to forget. The planning runs on your device, so the numbers are there the first time you open it, every time after.

03

Small, and done well

Each app does one thing and stays light. Amortiq plans loans; it isn't a bank. The game will be a game. Free to use, paid for by ads, with no feeds and no endless scroll pulling at your attention.

We collect nothing. No analytics or crash reporting of our own — we never see your data.

Ads come from Google. AdMob gets an ad ID, your IP and device info to show them. Nobody else does.

Your loan data stays put. It's encrypted on your phone and never leaves it for us or anyone.
